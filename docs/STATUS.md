## Scope and next action

The owner's goal is to finish the missing functionality, operations, and UI from the local concept/follow-up drafts, with accepted tracked decisions taking precedence. The goal is not complete. M0–M7 foundations are implemented; the active work is UX/functionality improvement, not another launch-preparation milestone.

APNG, GIF and WebP same-format saves preserve animation. AVIF sequence export, including one-frame sequences, preserves timed color/alpha tracks and repetition; still/animated display, previews and saving respect clean-aperture cropping and quarter-turn/reflection orientation. Static AVIF saves preserve edited RGBA8, including conversion from other static image formats. Next: image performance/actual-input validation and remaining AVIF derived-grid/premultiplied-alpha coverage. The captured Shell crash remains open, but the small apartment-lifetime comparison did not reproduce it; do not replace the worker architecture on that hypothesis alone. Fractional-pixel apertures, animation cross-format policy, long-animation cost and GIF palette quality/metadata also remain.

Publication, signing, and release recertification are deferred. An eventual assisted per-user installer is the accepted format, but not authorization to publish now. Windows 10 physical testing is optional; no installation into the owner's environment for that purpose. The owner permits visible-window interaction and clipboard use for scoped work, not unrelated writes or input.
