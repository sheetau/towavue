# Lucide icons

The demo's book-open, play, pause, repeat, repeat-1, shuffle, and volume-x paths come from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/), pinned to commit `ba6751ac45d379f6359d75ef2228cff6ba96c122`, matching the application's transport artwork.

Source: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/ba6751ac45d379f6359d75ef2228cff6ba96c122/icons

The JSX combines SVG path elements without changing their geometry. Strokes render at one CSS pixel; only play/pause shapes are filled. The full upstream license is in [lucide-LICENSE](lucide-LICENSE).

The tab-close and window-caption SVGs instead come from the owner's monapad LP. The towavue logo comes from the owner's plan.
